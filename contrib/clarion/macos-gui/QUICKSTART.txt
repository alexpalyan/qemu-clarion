QY8 Emulator: quick guide

Start
  1. Right-click QY8 Emulator.app, choose Open (first time only; macOS asks once).
  2. Pick your card image (.img), then your NAND dump (.bin).
  3. Wait about a minute for the Nissan logo, then the map or menu.

Touch
  Click the screen to tap. After about 10 taps on one screen the unit stops
  responding: go back a screen and return, or use the keys below.

Keys (click the window first)
  F1        Menu
  F2, Esc   Back
  F3        Display off/on (hold 2-3 s)
  F4        Map / current position
  F5        Camera        F6   Audio
  F7 / F8   Previous / next track
  Enter     Select (TUNE knob push)
  PageUp    Up in lists, zoom out on the map
  PageDown  Down in lists, zoom in on the map
  Wheel     Same as PageUp / PageDown
  = / -     Volume up / down

If something looks wrong
  Black screen after the logos   Hold F3, or press F4.
  "Touch OK to accept"           Click OK or press Enter.
  "Navigation Launching Failed"  The card and NAND come from different units.

Quit
  Close the window. Your card image is never changed.
